# GRiDD Technologies – Features & Benefits
### Version B1 – Unified GRiDD Voice / Corporate Professional

## Overview

GRiDD Technologies delivers the digital foundation for the global ground transportation industry through two integrated solutions:

1. **GNet Platform** – A cloud-based connectivity framework that powers farm-in/farm-out booking exchange, status synchronization, and live trip tracking between partners.  
2. **GNet Connect** – A content and discovery platform that enables operators and travel retailers to publish services, manage documents, and form verified affiliate relationships.

Together, they provide the infrastructure and visibility needed to unify ground transportation for travel retailers, operators, and technology partners.

---

## GNet Platform – The Infrastructure Layer

### Core Capabilities
- **Farm-in/Farm-out Booking Exchange** – Automates two-way reservation data sharing between connected partners.  
- **Status & Event Synchronization** – Updates bookings in real time (confirmation, en route, on-location, completed).  
- **Live Tracking** – GPS-based trip visibility for retailers and dispatch systems.  
- **Universal APIs** – Standardized REST/JSON endpoints for dispatch and booking platforms.  
- **Error Handling & Recovery** – Built-in validation and retry mechanisms ensure reliability.  
- **Scalable Cloud Infrastructure** – Microsoft Azure-hosted for global uptime and data security.  

### Benefits for Partners
- Simplifies integration across disparate dispatch systems.  
- Reduces operational costs by eliminating manual data entry.  
- Improves transparency and traveler satisfaction through real-time updates.  
- Enables standardized, compliant data exchange across networks.  

### Target Users
- Travel Retailers, OTAs, and TMCs seeking seamless ground integration.  
- Fleet Operators managing affiliate transactions.  
- Technology Vendors building or extending dispatch platforms.  

---

## GNet Connect – The Visibility Layer

### Core Capabilities
- **Public Operator Profiles** – Searchable listings with fleet, services, and location coverage.  
- **Affiliate Relationship Management** – Connect, verify, and manage affiliate networks.  
- **Document Management System** – Upload and share COIs, permits, and compliance files.  
- **Partner Discovery** – Intelligent search filters by service, location, and rating.  
- **Shoppable Content Framework** – Operators can present fleets and services in a bookable format.  
- **Analytics Dashboard** – Visibility into document activity and partner engagement.  

### Benefits for Partners
- Expands reach to verified global partners.  
- Simplifies compliance and pre-qualification.  
- Builds brand visibility through SEO-optimized company profiles.  
- Centralizes affiliate management and communications.  

### Target Users
- Fleet Operators aiming to grow their affiliate base.  
- Travel Retailers sourcing new, trusted ground providers.  
- Industry Networks and Associations promoting verified members.
